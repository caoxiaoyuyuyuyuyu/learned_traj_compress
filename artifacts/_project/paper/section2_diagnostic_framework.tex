\section{Diagnostic Framework}
\label{sec:framework}

The diagnostic protocol proceeds in two phases. Phase~1 (\S\ref{sec:framework}) localises the failure through three progressively narrower analyses; Phase~2 (\S\ref{sec:distillation}) tests causal interventions distinguishing strategy acquisition from execution capability.

\paragraph{Phase 1: collapse $\to$ geometry $\to$ activations.}
We evaluate the MEM1 RL policy (Qwen2.5-7B + PPO, trained via veRL~\cite{sheng2024verl}) and the Qwen2.5-7B base on a multi-objective RAG QA task from FlashRAG~\cite{jin2025flashrag}; each prompt contains $N$ questions ($N\in\{1,2,4,8,12,16\}$) and the agent runs \texttt{think}$\to$\texttt{search}$\to$\texttt{information}$\to$\texttt{answer} with per-objective normalized EM (em\_partial). Three signals together localise the gap. \textbf{(1a)~Collapse curve.} MEM1's em\_partial decreases monotonically from $0.62$ at $N\!=\!1$ to $0.341$ at $N\!=\!16$ (a 45\% drop, $\text{EM}\!\approx\!0.62-0.018N$, $R^2\!=\!0.984$, Cohen's $d\!=\!0.97$, no inflection point); the base model collapses immediately ($0.30$ at $N\!=\!1$, $0.075$--$0.148$ for $N\!\geq\!2$), so the distillation target is slope reduction rather than cliff recovery (Figure~\ref{fig:collapse_curve}). \textbf{(1b)~Weight geometry (descriptive observational context).} Across all 196 weight matrices of Qwen2.5-7B we measure the alignment ratio $r(k)\!=\!\alpha_\text{obs}(k)/\alpha_\text{rand}(k)$ between fine-tuning deltas $\Delta\mathbf{W}\!=\!\mathbf{W}_\text{ft}-\mathbf{W}_\text{base}$ and the top-$k$ base singular subspace, with $\alpha_\text{rand}$ calibrated against $n\!=\!100$ Gaussian matrices matched in shape and variance. We \emph{observe} that RL--base alignment is $2$--$6\!\times$ supra-random (e.g.\ $6.48\!\times$ at $k\!=\!64$, $2.78\!\times$ at $k\!=\!256$), whereas same-architecture full-rank SFT--base reaches only $1.47\!\times$ at $k\!=\!256$ ($2.92\!\times$ at $k\!=\!64$); a Wilcoxon signed-rank test gives $p\!<\!1.3\!\times\!10^{-32}$ at every $k$ and $99\%$ of matrices have $r_\text{RL}\!>\!r_\text{SFT}$ (Figure~\ref{fig:svd_boxplot}; full per-layer / U--V decomposition in Appendix~\ref{app:svd_details}). We treat this geometric signal as a descriptive correlate that motivates the causal interventions of \S\ref{sec:distillation}; the causal role of retrieval execution is established by the oracle ablation, not by this observation. \textbf{(1c)~Activation probing (negative).} Neither linear probes nor 2-layer MLPs ($n\!=\!300$ prompts, 6 layers, $k\!\in\!\{64,256\}$) recover a per-objective signal from $\Delta\mathbf{h}_l\!=\!\mathbf{h}_l^\text{RL}-\mathbf{h}_l^\text{base}$: linear binary accuracy $0.72\!<\!0.76$ majority-class, MLP $<$ linear in 47/55 cases ($p\!=\!4.83\!\times\!10^{-11}$), and 94.5\% of real $|\rho|$ values fall within a random-projection noise band (Appendix~\ref{app:probing}). The bottleneck thus lives in weight-space geometry, not activations, motivating the causal interventions of \S\ref{sec:distillation}.
