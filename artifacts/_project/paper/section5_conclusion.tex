\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Agentic SFT distillation can produce students that pass format-level, tool-call structural checks---perfect tool-call syntax, balanced XML tags, near-perfect query similarity---yet retain less than 6\% of the teacher's task performance, and we show this collapse replicates across two backbones and two parameter scales (Qwen2.5-3B and Llama-3.2-1B, spanning two model families and a ${\sim}3\times$ size difference). We have proposed a diagnostic protocol to detect this silent failure mode before pipeline deployment. The protocol proceeds by elimination: characterize the collapse shape, analyze weight geometry for geometric gaps between RL and SFT, and apply causal interventions (oracle and BM25 retrieval) that distinguish strategy acquisition from execution capability. Because the protocol requires only inference-time ablations, it is transferable to any agentic distillation setting where the teacher's competence depends on tool execution, and its signals (truncation scaling, oracle recovery, surface-vs-content metric gap) reproduce across at least two distinct student backbones (\S\ref{sec:cross_backbone}).

Applied to MEM1 (7B RL $\to$ 3B SFT, with a cross-backbone Llama-1B replication), the protocol yields five findings: (1)~multi-objective performance degrades monotonically with no phase transition, setting slope reduction as the distillation target; (2)~RL weight changes concentrate $2$--$6\times$ supra-random along base principal directions, while SFT recovers only $1.47\times$---a geometric gap robust across 196 matrices ($p < 1.3 \times 10^{-32}$); (3)~DPO fails to close the gap ($+1.0$pp, power $> 0.99$ at 5pp), ruling out insufficient training signal; (4)~oracle retrieval recovers $+47.3$pp ($p < 0.0001$), causally confirming that behavioral mismatch accounts for ${\sim}86$--$88\%$ of the gap, with capacity contributing only ${\sim}12$--$14\%$---and even BM25 retrieval recovers $+6$--$12$pp, offering an immediately deployable mitigation; (5)~the silent-failure signature and behavioral-mismatch diagnosis replicate on Llama-3.2-1B---format-level indicators all at $1.00$, action-sequence accuracy $0.606$, BLEU $1.16$, ROUGE-L $0.21$, and oracle retrieval again dropping truncation from 29--86\% to 0\% while recovering em\_partial by $\approx$34pp at $N\!=\!8$---evidence that the finding generalises across model families and parameter scales (\S\ref{sec:cross_backbone}).

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Scope.}
All experiments use one RL teacher (MEM1, 7B) on one domain (multi-objective RAG QA). Student-side, we cover two backbones and two scales (Qwen2.5-3B and Llama-3.2-1B), spanning cross-family and a ${\sim}3\times$ size difference, and observe the same silent-failure signature on both (\S\ref{sec:cross_backbone}). The literature survey (\S\ref{sec:related}) suggests the silent failure mode likely extends further to other systems evaluated only by format-level, tool-call structural checks, but direct replication on other \emph{teachers} and benchmarks remains future work.

\paragraph{Capacity isolation.}
A 7B full-rank pooled SFT quantifies the capacity contribution at $\approx$12--14\% of the teacher--student gap across all $N$ (\S\ref{sec:capacity}); however, the training-method mismatch (full-rank vs.\ LoRA) makes this a best-effort bound.

\paragraph{DPO data scarcity.}
DPO uses only 176 matched pairs ($0.4\%$ perfect rate at $N\!=\!8$), but power exceeds $0.99$ for a 5pp effect (floor 2.7pp). Oracle retrieval uses teacher passages (upper bound); BM25 retrieval confirms the finding generalizes beyond the oracle setting. Training a 3B RL baseline and cross-task generalization remain as future work.
